Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

HenryFlow ("we," "us," or "our") builds business software including InvenHelper, Survey Flow, and FlowCRM. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website and products, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep this policy in plain English because we believe you should actually be able to understand it.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account for a HenryFlow product such as InvenHelper, we collect your name, email address, and a password. Passwords are stored securely and are never visible to us in plain text.

Business data you provide

When you use InvenHelper, you may upload or enter business data such as inventory items, stock levels, supplier details, and CSV files. This data belongs to you. We store it only to provide the service to you, and you can export it as a CSV file or request its deletion at any time.

Contact and communication data

When you submit our contact form, we receive the email address, subject, and message you provide. Contact form submissions are delivered to us through FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service, and are used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure may automatically log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, which is used for security and to keep the site running reliably.

2. Survey Flow: On-Premises by Design

Survey Flow is installed and operated entirely on your facility's own hardware. All document processing and AI analysis happens locally, offline, inside your building. HenryFlow does not receive, store, or have access to any patient data, facility documents, or compliance records processed by Survey Flow. This architecture is intentional and supports HIPAA and other regulatory requirements that apply to healthcare facilities.

3. AI Features

InvenHelper includes an AI assistant and an AI-assisted CSV import feature. To provide these features, relevant inventory data from your account may be processed by our AI service provider (OpenAI) at the moment you use the feature. This data is used only to generate your answer or process your import — we do not permit our AI providers to use your data to train their models.

Survey Flow's AI runs entirely on local hardware within your facility and sends nothing to any external AI provider.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our products and website
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • To respond to your questions, demo requests, and support inquiries
  • To notify you about important product updates or changes to our services
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents

5. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal information or business data — to anyone, ever
  • We do not share your data with advertisers
  • We do not use your business data for any purpose other than providing the service to you

6. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted service providers to operate our website and products:

  • Cloud hosting — our website and InvenHelper's encrypted cloud storage
  • FormSubmit — delivery of contact form messages
  • OpenAI — powering InvenHelper's AI assistant and smart CSV import
  • GitHub — our public development activity feed shown on product update pages

Each provider receives only the minimum information necessary to perform its function.

7. Data Security

We protect your information with industry-standard measures, including encrypted storage, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and email/password authentication on all accounts. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data using reasonable and appropriate safeguards.

8. Data Retention, Export, and Deletion

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active. You may export your InvenHelper data as a CSV file at any time. If you close your account or ask us to delete your data, we will remove it from our systems within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

9. Children's Privacy

Our products and website are intended for business use and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated to account holders by email or an in-product notice.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out through our contact page and we'll respond promptly.
