HenryFlow is a software company that builds practical tools for small and mid-sized businesses — InvenHelper for inventory management, Survey Flow for healthcare compliance, and FlowCRM (in development) for customer relationship management.
We keep it simple: each product is a single, flat monthly subscription. There are no tiers to compare, no per-seat surprises, and no hidden fees. Contact us for current pricing on the product you're interested in.
