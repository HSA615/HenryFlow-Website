Capabilities

Intelligent compliance management

Survey Flow streamlines the complexity of healthcare surveys by combining local AI automation with professional human oversight.

100% On-Premises Security

Survey Flow runs entirely inside your facility, air-gapped from the internet. Sensitive patient and facility data never leaves your building.

Automated Classification

The AI engine handles the filing and renaming of compliance PDFs, keeping your documentation organized without manual effort.

Instant Report Population

Automatically link supporting evidence to AHCA and Joint Commission forms with clickable PDF hyperlinks — no more manual searching.

Proactive Deficiency Detection

Identify missing signatures, expired records, and compliance gaps before a surveyor cites them, with your facility score updated in real time.

One-Click Evidence Binders

Generate complete, professional evidence binders for surveyors instantly — the finalized report plus all referenced documentation in one package.

Verified Audit Trails

Secure, tamper-evident logs record every action in the system, so every document change is traceable and verifiable.

Why Survey Flow

Expert Oversight
