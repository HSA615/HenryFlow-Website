Version 2.0

Major Release

Latest stable release — June 2026

Local AI Integration (Ollama)

Migrated from cloud-based analysis to a fully local LLM engine. Your data now never leaves the facility, supporting complete HIPAA and regulatory compliance.

Intelligent Report Population

New capability to automatically populate AHCA and Joint Commission reports with clickable PDF hyperlinks. No more manual searching for supporting evidence.

Automated Deficiency Detection

The AI now proactively flags missing signatures, expired documents, and compliance gaps, updating your facility score in real time.

Evidence Binder Generation

Introduced one-click export for evidence binders, bundling the finalized report with all referenced supporting documentation in a structured ZIP/PDF package.

Secure Activity Logging

Implemented advanced, tamper-evident audit logs to ensure every document action is recorded and verifiable.
