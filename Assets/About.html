About HenryFlow

Professional-grade tools for growing businesses

We build the software small businesses deserve — capable, secure, and refreshingly simple.

Our Mission

Level the playing field

HenryFlow was founded on a simple belief: small businesses deserve the same powerful tools as large corporations. We specialize in creating intuitive, scalable, high-performance software that eliminates administrative friction — so entrepreneurs can focus on what they do best: growing their business.

Our Vision

One ecosystem, built product by product

Our goal is a comprehensive suite of business products that work in harmony — from the first lead captured in FlowCRM, to the daily stock check in InvenHelper, to the compliance binder generated in Survey Flow. We provide the digital infrastructure for modern operations, one product at a time.

What We Stand For

Our values

Quality Over Quantity

We would rather ship one product that works flawlessly than five that almost do. Every feature earns its place.

Respect for Your Data

Your business data belongs to you. We design for security first, make export easy, and never sell or share customer information.

Honest Communication

Straightforward pricing, real release notes, and direct answers. If something is still in development, we say so.

Leadership

Meet the founder

Henry Alberts is a driven entrepreneur dedicated to supporting the small business community. With a passion for efficiency and a commitment to quality, Henry leads the development of every HenryFlow product to ensure it meets the rigorous needs of real-world business owners.
